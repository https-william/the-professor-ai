Explain the concept of superposition simply.

The Professor 10:24 AM

Superposition is a fundamental principle of quantum mechanics. Think of it like a spinning coin.

  • While spinning, it's not strictly heads or tails—it's a mix of both states at once.
  • It only becomes heads or tails when you stop it (measure it).

In physics terms, it states that any two (or more) quantum states can be added together ('superposed') and the result will be another valid quantum state.

|Ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩
